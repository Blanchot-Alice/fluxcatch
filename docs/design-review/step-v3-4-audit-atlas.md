## What changed · 改了什么

### §3.1 acceptance in the audit · 审计里的第 3.1 节验收

- `scripts/lib/pond-metrics.mjs` (new, pure, unit-tested): `pondMetrics` compares the capture with the capture taken with the layer hidden (the audit already took that pair), in OKLCH through the module's own `rgbToOklch`: chroma coverage is the share of sampled pixels whose Oklab a/b distance from the no-pond pixel exceeds 0.015; p95 |ΔL| is the 95th percentile of the lightness difference over every sampled pixel of the region (unchanged pixels count as 0, as in the reference script's full-page numbers). The region is the page's margins outside `main` on full pages, else the whole frame; rects that update by themselves (progress, live regions, toasts) are skipped as before.
  `scripts/lib/pond-metrics.mjs`（新增，纯函数，有单测）：`pondMetrics` 把截图和隐藏图层后拍的同一张截图（审计本来就拍这一对）放到 OKLCH 里比较，用的是模块自己的 `rgbToOklch`：色度覆盖率是采样像素中与无池塘像素在 Oklab a/b 平面距离超过 0.015 的比例；p95 |ΔL| 是区域内全部采样像素明度差的第 95 百分位（未变化的像素按 0 计，与参考脚本整页数字的算法一致）。区域在整页上是 `main` 之外的两侧留白，其它界面是整帧；自己会变的矩形（进度、live 区域、吐司）照旧跳过。
- `pondVerdict`: on a judged screen, coverage inside 3% to 12% and p95 |ΔL| at most 0.03 in Morning and 0.05 in Dusk; on every screen with the layer, no text run with pond alpha over 0.02 under it (the mask rule, measured as described under "contrast"). The report's `pond` column reads "coverage · p95", in parentheses where the screen is reported but not judged.
  `pondVerdict`：判定屏上覆盖率须在 3% 到 12% 之间，p95 |ΔL| 晨色不超过 0.03、暮色不超过 0.05；凡有图层的界面，任何文字行下方池塘 alpha 不得超过 0.02（遮罩规则，量法见“对比度”）。报告的 `pond` 列写作“覆盖率 · p95”，只汇报不判定的界面加括号。

It is information, not a verdict: how far the band moves with the per-install seed.

### Contrast against the composited background · 对合成背景的对比度

- `scripts/lib/page-audit.mjs` reads the pond canvas once (drawn into an OffscreenCanvas, since the layer's own context is the page's). For every text run whose ancestor backgrounds leave the canvas showing, it samples the layer's pixels under each line box (up to about 4 096 per box), composites each sample over the canvas and the translucent ancestor stack, and takes the worst contrast ratio; text over an opaque ancestor is unchanged. The record carries `pondAlpha`, the largest alpha found under the run; `contrast.pondUnderText` lists runs over 0.02.
  `scripts/lib/page-audit.mjs` 读取一次池塘画布（画进 OffscreenCanvas，因为图层自己的上下文归页面所有）。凡祖先背景透出画布的文字行，在每个行框下采样图层像素（每框最多约 4 096 个），每个样本先叠在画布上、再叠半透明的祖先栈，取最差的对比度；位于不透明祖先上的文字不变。记录带 `pondAlpha`，即该行下方找到的最大 alpha；`contrast.pondUnderText` 列出超过 0.02 的行。
- Result on this run: see "Tests and checks". The point of the check is the §6 rule "never place the pond under text without the mask": it now fails the audit the moment a page's content-box list misses a text block.
  本次运行的结果见“测试与检查”。这项检查对应第 6 节“不得把池塘不加遮罩地放在文字下面”：一旦某页的内容框列表漏掉一段文字，审计立刻失败。

### The header at scroll 0 · 滚动位置 0 的页眉

- In the page: `result.header` asserts, at `scrollY` 0 with no scrolled element, that `.topbar` has background alpha 0 and no box-shadow, and that its `::before` and `::after` show neither a band (opacity × background alpha) nor a shadow. The computed opacity of the band under the scroll-driven animation is 0 at scroll 0, and 0 when the page cannot scroll at all (the timeline is inactive, the base value applies).
  页面内：`result.header` 在 `scrollY` 为 0、无元素滚动时断言 `.topbar` 背景 alpha 为 0、无阴影，`::before` 与 `::after` 既不显示色带（不透明度 × 背景 alpha）也不显示阴影。滚动驱动动画下色带在滚动位置 0 的计算不透明度为 0，页面根本不能滚动时也是 0（时间线不活动，用基础值）。
- In the driver: `headerStrip` checks the capture taken with the layer hidden: the bar's rect minus its rendered children (the inner column; in the popup also the view bar) must be the canvas colour within 2/255 per channel. Both go into the `header` column; a dialog's backdrop skips the pixel check.

## Interpretations and deviations · 解读与偏离

1. **The seed is pinned, to the module's own fallback.** The audit stores `0x5EED1E5` before each run. That is the value `pond.js` uses when storage is unavailable, so it is a seed the module names itself rather than one picked for the audit; `--pond-seed` runs any other. Without a pinned seed each audit profile drew a fresh random seed and the pond column could not be compared between runs (step 2's metrics showed Dusk at 2.2 % on one profile and 7.7 % on another).
   **seed 固定为模块自带的回退值。** 审计每次运行前写入 `0x5EED1E5`。这是 `pond.js` 在存储不可用时自己使用的值，所以它是模块自己点名的 seed，而非为审计挑选的；`--pond-seed` 可以换任何别的。不固定的话，每个审计配置都会抽一个新的随机 seed，pond 列在两次运行之间无从比较（第 2 步的指标里暮色在一个配置下 2.2%、另一个配置下 7.7%）。
2. **Only full pages with gutters of 48 px or more are judged.** At 960 wide the gutters are 40 px, inside the 64 px feather, so the band cannot hold there by construction (0.1 % to 0.5 % in step 2); the popup and the side panel have no margins at all. Those screens are measured and printed in parentheses; the mask rule (no pond under text) still applies to them and is judged.
   **只判定留白不少于 48 px 的整页。** 960 宽时留白 40 px，落在 64 px 的羽化区内，区间在那里按构造就不可能成立（第 2 步里 0.1% 到 0.5%）；弹窗和侧边栏根本没有留白。这些界面照样度量，数字加括号列出；遮罩规则（文字下无池塘）对它们仍然适用，并照常判定。
3. **The 4 % floor was not met everywhere at the pinned seed, the layer was not tuned to meet it, and the floor is now 3 % as seen.** The preview's Morning margins (3.2 % to 3.9 %) and the whole Settings page in Dusk (3.8 %) missed 4 %. Three things are measurable about why. The layer is planned per viewport bucket, so the same seed composes differently on each page size: the Settings first screen gives 4.8 % in Morning and the whole page 4.1 %. The mask takes the feather's share off every margin: for the pinned seed at 1280 × 900 the unmasked margins carry 6.9 % (Morning) and 6.6 % (Dusk), as seen the Downloads page shows 4.7 % and 4.8 %, so about seven tenths survive. And the spread across seeds is wide (the sweep). The reference seed itself gives 4.4 % unmasked, which masking alone brings under 4 %. By §6 none of this was tuned in the layer: the feather and the density live in `pond_layer.py` and are unchanged. Of the three ways out (keep 4 % as a property of the reference seed at 1280 × 900 only; lower the floor for margins as seen; raise the margin density or shorten the feather in the script), Andy chose the second on 2026-10-24: the floor for margins as seen is 3 %, the review's acceptance sentence records the change and its date, `POND_BAND.coverageMin` is 3, and the verdicts of this run were recomputed from its own measurements rather than re-captured.
